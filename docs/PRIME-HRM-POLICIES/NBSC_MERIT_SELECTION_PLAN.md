# Northern Bukidnon State College — Institutional Merit Selection Plan (MSP)

## Purpose and Scope
This Merit Selection Plan (MSP) governs the recruitment, screening, evaluation, selection, and appointment of all academic teaching personnel and administrative non-teaching employees of Northern Bukidnon State College (NBSC), Manolo Fortich, Bukidnon.

## Recruitment & Hiring Pipeline Stages
All job applications must advance through the 8 sequential milestones:
1. **Stage 1: APPLIED (Online Submission)**: Candidate submits Personal Data Sheet (CS Form 212 Revised 2017), Transcript of Records, and relevant credentials through the Career Job Board.
2. **Stage 2: SCREENING (Documentary Verification)**: Human Resource Management Office (HRMO) validates minimum Qualification Standards (QS) against CSC rules.
3. **Stage 3: DSS_SCORED (4-Pillar Scoring)**: Automated and objective computation of Merit, Competence, Ethics, and Service Orientation ratings.
4. **Stage 4: DEPT_EVALUATION (Department Head Review)**: Supervising Dean or Institute Director conducts a structured 5-point Likert rubric evaluation and teaching demonstration.
5. **Stage 5: DELIBERATION (HRMPSB Board Assessment)**: The Human Resource Merit Promotion and Selection Board deliberates on qualified candidates.
6. **Stage 6: APPOINTMENT_ISSUED (Executive Selection)**: The College President reviews top five ranked candidates and executes the official appointment resolution.
7. **Stage 7: DOCUMENT_VERIFICATION (Compliance Audit)**: Formal submission of medical certificate, NBI clearance, oath of office, and PDS notarization.
8. **Stage 8: ONBOARDED (Plantilla Induction)**: 201 Personnel File creation, orientation on institutional mandates, and payroll inclusion.

## HRMPSB Board Deliberation & Voting Protocol
1. **Composition**:
   - HR Administrative Officer (Chairperson)
   - Respective Institute Director / Dean (Vice Chairperson)
   - Accredited Faculty Association Representative
   - Administrative Staff Union Representative
   - Two Designated Subject-Matter Specialists
2. **Quorum & Consensus**:
   - A minimum of 50% + 1 of official board members must be in attendance for a valid quorum.
   - Each member casts a confidential digital ballot (`Approve`, `Disapprove`, `Abstain`) alongside priority ranking notes.
   - The top five candidates who meet or exceed the 75.00 benchmark score are transmitted to the Office of the College President for final appointment.

## Cryptographic Audit Trail Guarantee
Pursuant to Republic Act No. 8792 (Electronic Commerce Act) and NBSC Transparency Governance:
- Every stage transition, DSS score computation, board vote, and final appointment resolution is hashed using SHA-256 and committed to the immutable cryptographic audit chain.
- The audit ledger is accessible in real-time by institutional stakeholders for integrity verification.
