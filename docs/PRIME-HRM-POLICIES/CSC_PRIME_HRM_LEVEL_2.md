# Civil Service Commission PRIME-HRM Level 2 Accreditation Standards

## Institutional Authority & Regulatory Framework
This standard is established pursuant to Civil Service Commission (CSC) Memorandum Circular No. 19, Series of 2012, and CSC Resolution No. 1800692 on the Equal Employment Opportunity Principle (EEOP) and Competency-Based Human Resource Management Systems.

## Pillar I: Recruitment, Selection, and Placement (RSP)
1. **Merit & Competence Standard**: All appointments in Northern Bukidnon State College (NBSC) shall be strictly based on merit and fitness determined through competitive selection procedures.
2. **Four-Pillar Decision Support System (DSS)**:
   - **Merit (30% default)**: Evaluates certified educational attainment from recognized higher education institutions, verified years of specialized work experience, and accredited professional training hours.
   - **Competence (30% default)**: Assessed via formal teaching demonstrations and structured Behavioral Event Interviews (BEI) utilizing standardized rubric anchors.
   - **Ethics (20% default)**: Verified through exhaustive character background investigations, ombudsman clearance checks, and reference verifications.
   - **Service Orientation (20% default)**: Quantifies past community extension involvement, institutional citizenship, and civic volunteer leadership.
3. **Equal Employment Opportunity Principle (EEOP)**:
   - NBSC upholds an open, non-discriminatory recruitment policy regardless of age, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, civil status, disability, religion, ethnicity, or political affiliation.
   - Special accommodations and assistive technology shall be provided to persons with disabilities (PWD) during examinations and interview sessions.

## Pillar II: Performance Management (PM)
1. **Strategic Performance Management System (SPMS)**:
   - Aligns individual employee performance commitments with NBSC's institutional Key Result Areas (KRAs).
   - Semi-annual review using the Individual Performance Commitment and Review (IPCR) and Office Performance Commitment and Review (OPCR) instruments.
2. **Performance Rating Scale**:
   - 5.00: Outstanding
   - 4.00 – 4.99: Very Satisfactory
   - 3.00 – 3.99: Satisfactory (minimum threshold for merit promotion)
   - 2.00 – 2.99: Unsatisfactory
   - Below 2.00: Poor

## Pillar III: Learning and Development (L&D)
1. **Core Competency Profiling**: Identifies targeted skill enhancements across teaching pedagogical mastery, scientific research publication, and administrative automation.
2. **Mandatory Continuous Professional Development (CPD)**: Minimum requirement of 16 training hours per calendar year for permanent and tenure-track personnel.

## Pillar IV: Rewards and Recognition (R&R)
1. **Program on Awards and Incentives for Service Excellence (PRAISE)**:
   - Annual citation awards: Best Performing Faculty, Outstanding Research Innovator, Exemplary Administrative Aide.
   - Monetary incentives, educational grants, and fast-track promotion points.
