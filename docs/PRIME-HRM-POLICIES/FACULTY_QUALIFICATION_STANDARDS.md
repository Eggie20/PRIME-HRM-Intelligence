# NBSC Faculty & Staff Qualification Standards (QS) Matrix

## Regulatory Authority
Formulated in accordance with the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) Memorandum Order No. 40, Series of 2008, and the Civil Service Commission (CSC) 2017 Omnibus Rules on Appointments and Other Human Resource Actions (ORAOHRA).

## Academic Teaching Faculty Positions

### 1. Instructor I (Salary Grade 12)
- **Education**: Master's degree in the discipline or relevant allied field from an accredited institution. (For Contract of Service with pending thesis, must have completed at least 24 academic units towards a master's degree).
- **Experience**: 1 year of relevant tertiary teaching or industry professional experience.
- **Training**: 4 hours of relevant training in instructional delivery, curriculum design, or educational technology.
- **Eligibility**: RA 1080 (Board Licensure Examination for Professional Teachers - LET) or Career Service Professional / PRC Professional Licensure.

### 2. Assistant Professor II (Salary Grade 16)
- **Education**: Earned Master's degree in the discipline of appointment.
- **Experience**: 2 years of tertiary teaching experience, demonstrated academic mentoring, or peer-reviewed research output.
- **Training**: 8 hours of relevant advanced training in pedagogical assessment, research methods, or academic governance.
- **Eligibility**: RA 1080 or Career Service Professional eligibility.

### 3. Associate Professor IV (Salary Grade 21)
- **Education**: Master's degree and earned doctoral units (minimum 18 units) in the relevant field of specialization.
- **Experience**: 3 years of tertiary academic experience with demonstrated research publications in indexed or peer-reviewed journals.
- **Training**: 16 hours of specialized training in research leadership, institutional accreditation, or higher education administration.
- **Eligibility**: RA 1080 or Career Service Professional.

### 4. Full Professor (Salary Grade 24–26)
- **Education**: Earned Doctorate Degree (Ph.D., Ed.D., D.M.) from a recognized institution.
- **Experience**: Minimum 5 years of tertiary teaching, documented Scopus/WoS indexed research publications, and national community extension leadership.
- **Training**: 24 hours of executive leadership and institutional strategic management training.
- **Eligibility**: RA 1080 or Career Service Professional.

## Non-Teaching Administrative Positions

### 1. Administrative Aide VI (Salary Grade 6)
- **Education**: Completion of 2 years of tertiary studies or high school graduate with a 2-year accredited technical-vocational diploma.
- **Experience**: 1 year of relevant clerical, secretarial, or digital records management experience.
- **Training**: 4 hours of relevant training in office automation and records safekeeping.
- **Eligibility**: Career Service Subprofessional (First Level Eligibility).

### 2. Administrative Officer V / HRMO (Salary Grade 18)
- **Education**: Bachelor's Degree in Human Resource Management, Public Administration, Psychology, or Business Administration.
- **Experience**: 2 years of relevant supervisory experience in human resource systems or public sector personnel administration.
- **Training**: 8 hours of relevant management and supervisory training.
- **Eligibility**: Career Service Professional (Second Level Eligibility).
