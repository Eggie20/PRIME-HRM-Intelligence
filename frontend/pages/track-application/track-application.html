📋 Merit Selection Stages

  • 1. Application Docketing: System logs submitted packet and assigns unique tracking ID.
  • 2. Document & QS Screening: HRMO audits education, experience, and eligibility compliance.
  • 3. 4-Pillar DSS Scoring: Automated multi-attribute benchmarking and comparative ranking.
  • 4. Dept Head Demonstration: Teaching demonstration rubric and specialized interview.
  • 5. HRMPSB Deliberation: Selection board comparative consensus and final ranking.
  • 6. President Appointment: Issuance of official appointment under Civil Service rules.
  • 7. Board Attestation: Submission of resolution and plantilla assignment to CSC.
  • 8. Assumption to Duty: Formal oath administration and institutional onboarding.
